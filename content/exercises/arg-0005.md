The Larkspur Prize, an arts council's award for the year's best new play, has never gone to a
production that ran for fewer than six weeks. The council's judges have said that they want to see how
a play holds up over a long run. This season, the Hollow Lantern company's new play has just completed
its full booked run of eight weeks at the Corbin Street theater. Since the play clears the bar that every
past winner has met, the company's board is right to expect that it will win this year's Larkspur Prize.
