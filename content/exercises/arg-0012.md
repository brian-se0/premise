Two years ago the city of Marrow installed speed cameras on eight streets where residents had complained
about speeding. Since then, injury crashes on those eight streets have fallen by 40 percent. Over the same
period, injury crashes on Marrow's other streets fell by only 5 percent, and a separate analysis ruled out
citywide changes, such as safer cars or milder weather, as the main cause of the decline on the eight
streets. The city council concludes that the cameras are responsible for most of the decline in injury
crashes on the eight streets.
